# Staff Clicks | Virtual Assistance & Business Process Outsourcing

Official modern web application for **Staff Clicks**, a premium Virtual Assistance & Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) company.

## 🏢 Business Overview

- **Company Name:** Staff Clicks
- **Direct Hotline:** +91 83026 48461
- **Email:** contact@staffclicks.com
- **Delivery & Operations Hub:** Ajmer, Rajasthan, India
- **Primary Client Markets:** India, USA, and Canada

## 🎨 Brand Design System

- **Primary Color:** `#093965` (Deep Navy)
- **Secondary Color:** `#2E8D9F` (Teal)
- **Accent Color:** `#FA7D3C` (Warm Orange)
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Animations:** Framer Motion & CSS Micro-Interactions
- **Routing:** React Router v7

## 🧭 Routes & Architecture

- `/` — Homepage with Hero, Value Strip, About Preview, Services Matrix, How It Works, Why Staff Clicks, Global Cross-Border Map, Outsourcing Benefits, and FAQ
- `/about` — About Us, Operations Hub in Ajmer, Mission, Vision, and SLA Standards
- `/services` — All 9 Service Categories with Domain Filters
  - `/services/virtual-assistance` — Virtual Assistance
  - `/services/recruitment` — Recruitment Services
  - `/services/customer-support` — Customer Support
  - `/services/data-management` — Data & CRM Management
  - `/services/lead-generation` — Lead Generation
  - `/services/administrative-support` — Administrative Support
  - `/services/accounting-bookkeeping` — Accounting & Bookkeeping Support
  - `/services/ecommerce-support` — E-commerce Support
  - `/services/insurance-support` — Insurance Back-Office Support
- `/industries` — 10 Dedicated Verticals (Insurance, Finance, E-commerce, Real Estate, Healthcare, Tech, Marketing, Professional Services, Staffing, SMBs)
- `/why-staff-clicks` — Value Proposition, Freelancer vs. Managed BPO Comparison
- `/careers` — Talent Network, Work Culture & Resume Submission Form
- `/contact` — Contact Details, Service Inquiries, and Interactive Request Form
- `/faq` — Searchable Knowledge Base & Questions
- `/privacy-policy` & `/terms-of-service` — Regulatory & Compliance Documents

## 💻 Development & Build
